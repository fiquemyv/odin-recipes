Posho Recipe

Description

A hearty Ugandan meal featuring soft posho served with richly spiced fish, fresh greens, and a touch of lime for a flavorful, satisfying finish.

Ingredients

  • Whole fish (1 pound)
  • Tomatoes (2 medium)
  • Onions (1 large)
  • Garlic (2 cloves)
  • Fresh leafy greens (1 cup)
  • Bell peppers (2 medium)
  • Cooking oil (2 tablespoons)
  • Fresh herbs (1 tablespoon)
  • Lime (1 medium)
  • Salt and spices (to taste)

Steps

  1. Clean and season the fish with salt and spices.
  2. Heat oil in a pan and sauté onions, garlic, and bell peppers until soft.
  3. Add chopped tomatoes and cook until they form a sauce.
  4. Place the fish in the sauce and cook until done.
  5. Steam or boil the leafy greens until tender.
  6. Prepare posho by boiling water and gradually adding maize flour, stirring continuously until thickened.
  7. Serve the posho with the cooked fish, greens, and a squeeze of lime on top.
