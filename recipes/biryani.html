Biryani Recipe

Description

A flavorful and aromatic Indian dish made with basmati rice, tender meat, and a blend of spices, garnished with fried onions and fresh herbs.

Ingredients

  • Basmati rice (2 cups)
  • Chicken or lamb (1 pound)
  • Onions (2 large)
  • Garlic (3 cloves)
  • Ginger (1 inch piece)
  • Yogurt (1 cup)
  • Tomatoes (2 medium)
  • Cooking oil (3 tablespoons)
  • Biryani spices (1 tablespoon)
  • Saffron strands (a pinch)
  • Fresh cilantro and mint leaves (for garnish)
  • Salt and pepper (to taste)

Steps

  1. Rinse the basmati rice and soak it in water for 30 minutes.
  2. In a large pot, heat oil and sauté onions until golden brown. Remove half for garnish.
  3. Add garlic, ginger, and biryani spices to the pot and cook for a minute.
  4. Add the meat and cook until browned. Stir in yogurt and chopped tomatoes, cooking until the meat is tender.
  5. In a separate pot, bring water to a boil, add salt, and cook the soaked rice until it's 70% done. Drain the rice.
  6. Layer the partially cooked rice over the meat mixture in the pot. Sprinkle saffron strands and garnish with fried onions, cilantro, and mint leaves.
  7. Cover the pot tightly and cook on low heat for 20-25 minutes to allow flavors to meld.
  8. Serve hot with raita or salad on the side.
